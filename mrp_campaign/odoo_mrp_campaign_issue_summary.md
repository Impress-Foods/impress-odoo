# Odoo Manufacturing Order (MO) Scheduling Issue - `mrp_campaign` Module

## Date of Investigation: 2026-01-20

## Agent: Gemini CLI

---

## 1. Issue Description

The user reported that Odoo was "not respecting dependencies between MOs when trying to
plan them" when using the custom `mrp_campaign` module. Specifically, Manufacturing
Orders (MOs) for final products were being scheduled _before_ the intermediate "anchor"
products they depended on, leading to "out of order" planning and planning warnings in
Odoo. This occurred despite `stock.move` dependencies (`move_orig_ids` and
`move_dest_ids`) being correctly established. Lead times for all products were stated
as 0.

## 2. Evolution of Understanding

The investigation involved several hypotheses and clarifications from the user, each
revealing a deeper layer of the problem:

- **Initial Hypothesis (Agent):** `mrp_campaign`'s deferred MO creation and manual
  linking confused the scheduler.
  - **Correction (User):** The `stock.move` chain integrity was respected; the issue was
    that MOs were planned out of order, and Odoo itself warned of planning conflicts.
- **Second Hypothesis (Agent):** Manually triggering the scheduler
  (`_trigger_scheduler`) after linking would solve it.
  - **Correction (User):** The user manually "plans" MOs via a button, and Odoo's
    warnings persist.
- **Third Hypothesis (Agent):** Conflicting `date_start`/`date_deadline` values were
  causing the issue, specifically the anchor product MOs having deadlines extending to
  the next day while final products were constrained to the current day.
  - **Correction (User):** When MOs are _created_ (before any planning), all MOs are
    assigned `date_start` (beginning of day) and `date_deadline` (end of day) of the
    `campaign.date_planned_start`. No initial conflict exists. The observed date changes
    (e.g., `date_deadline` extending) happen _after_ planning.
- **Fourth Hypothesis (Agent):** The single-day window (24 hours) was too restrictive
  for Odoo's scheduler to fit all operations.
  - **Correction (User):** Odoo _can_ plan operations within a much smaller window
    (e.g., 3 hours). The issue isn't fitting, but _logical ordering_.
- **Fifth Hypothesis (Agent):** Odoo's scheduler, for zero-lead-time moves within a day,
  struggles with granular sequencing. Proposed staggering `date_start` by a minimal
  amount.
  - **Correction (User):** The staggering _was_ successfully applied by `mrp_campaign`
    after confirmation, but the "Plan" button action _overwrote_ or _ignored_ these
    staggered dates.
- **Sixth Hypothesis (Agent):** The `_plan_workorders` method (triggered by the "Plan"
  button) was aggressively recalculating and overwriting `date_start` (and
  `date_finished`). Also, `mrp.production.date_deadline` is a computed field based on
  `stock.move.date_deadline`.
  - **Correction (User):** After _recreating MOs without planning_, the staggered
    `date_start` was indeed visible. This confirmed that the staggering implementation
    worked, but was overridden by the subsequent "Plan` action. Staggering was removed.
- **Seventh Hypothesis (Agent):** The core problem was inconsistent `date_deadline`
  values on `stock.move` records, particularly for anchor product components. An
  observed `date_deadline` of `13:00:00` for an anchor component move (meaning it's
  needed by then) clashed with its own planned completion time (`date`) of `13:50:00`.
  This problematic `date_deadline` value (e.g., `13:00:00` or `13:21:36`) appeared to
  propagate from some earlier demand date.
  - **Critical User Constraint:** "Initial demand moves (such as those generated by a
    delivery order) shouldn't be modified, as their deadlines and planned dates are what
    drives the true timeline and needs to be synced with client communications and
    delivery vendors."
- **Eighth Hypothesis (Agent):** Odoo's scheduler, via `mrp.workorder._plan_workorder`,
  uses `max(self.production_id.date_start, datetime.now())` for a work order's
  `date_start`. This means if MOs are planned later in the day, work orders will not be
  scheduled to start before `datetime.now()`, even if the MO's `date_start` is earlier.
  Early `date_deadline` values for MOs (e.g., 1 AM) can be infeasible due to work center
  operating hours, leading to conflicts.
- **Ninth Hypothesis (Agent):** The Odoo scheduler actively modifies the `date_deadline`
  of related `stock.picking` records during MO planning. It attempts to back-schedule
  the delivery deadline to match the earliest possible completion of the MOs, even if
  this results in a delivery `date_deadline` that precedes the actual `scheduled_date`
  of the delivery. This creates an impossible delivery timeline from the perspective of
  external commitments. This dynamic modification of delivery `date_deadline` happens
  when `campaign.date_planned_start` is set to an earlier date (e.g.,
  `fields.Date.today()`) than the actual `scheduled_date` of the delivery order.
- **Tenth Hypothesis (Agent):** The `Procurement` object's `move_dest_ids`, when
  received by `mrp_campaign._collect_procurements`, can sometimes be empty or contain
  incomplete `date_deadline` information. This causes `campaign.date_planned_start` to
  default to `fields.Date.today()` (the campaign creation date) instead of aligning with
  the actual demand `date_deadline` from the Sales/Delivery Order. The
  `mrp.campaign.line.move_dest_ids` field is a more reliable source for demand dates
  after the `mrp.campaign.line` records have been created/updated.

## 3. Key Modules & Methods Involved

- **`mrp_campaign/__manifest__.py`**: Module definition and dependencies.
- **`mrp_campaign/models/mrp_campaign.py`**:
  - `MrpCampaign.action_confirm_bulk`: Central method for creating anchor MOs, linking
    moves, and applying initial date/stagger logic.
  - `MrpCampaignLine._create_finished_product_mo`: Method for creating final product
    MOs.
  - `MrpCampaign.write`: Logic for updating MO dates if campaign `date_planned_start`
    changes.
  - `MrpCampaign._sync_date_planned_start`: New method to synchronize
    `date_planned_start` and `bucket_start_date` with demand move deadlines.
  - `MrpCampaign._get_or_create_campaign_for_anchor`: Method to find or create a
    campaign, now calls `_sync_date_planned_start` after creation.
  - `MrpCampaign._collect_procurements`: Method to collect procurements, now calls
    `_sync_date_planned_start` after campaign line updates.
- **`mrp_campaign/models/stock_rule.py`**:
  - `StockRuleInherit._run_manufacture`: Intercepts standard procurement, diverting to
    campaign logic.
- **`/opt/odoo/addons/mrp/models/mrp_production.py` (Odoo Core)**:
  - `MrpProduction._get_default_date_start`: Default `date_start` (defaults to
    `datetime.now()`).
  - `MrpProduction._compute_date_deadline`: Computed field for MO `date_deadline`,
    derived from `move_finished_ids.date_deadline`.
  - `MrpProduction.button_plan`: Triggers scheduling.
  - `MrpProduction._plan_workorders`: Overwrites MO `date_start` (and `date_finished`)
    based on work order planning.
- **`/opt/odoo/addons/stock/models/stock_move.py` (Odoo Core)**:
  - `stock.move.date`: Planned execution date/time for the move.
  - `stock.move.date_deadline`: Hard deadline for the move.
  - `stock.move.move_orig_ids` / `stock.move.move_dest_ids`: Dependency links between
    moves.
- **`/opt/odoo/addons/mrp/models/mrp_workorder.py` (Odoo Core)**:
  - `MrpWorkorder._plan_workorder`: Core work order planning logic, uses
    `max(self.production_id.date_start, datetime.now())`.
